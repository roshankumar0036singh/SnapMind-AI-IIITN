How the Orvan grain corridor worked, and why its ending matters

[P1] The Corridor Agreement was a one-year arrangement signed on 10 September 2025. Asgard mediated it.

[P2] Orvan grows more wheat and maize than it eats and sells the surplus by sea from Orvan Grain Port. The agreement kept that sea route open to commercial ships.

[P3] Under the agreement, sea exports ran at about 95% of the old normal. The deal covered wheat and maize leaving the port by sea; it did not cover rail movements or sales by other exporters.

[P4] The agreement expired on 10 September 2026. Talks on renewal failed, and the port moved onto the general rules with no replacement arrangement.

[P5] The main alternative is rail. The Ravik Rail Link can carry grain to Ravik and onward, but its capacity is limited and it cannot move the volumes
