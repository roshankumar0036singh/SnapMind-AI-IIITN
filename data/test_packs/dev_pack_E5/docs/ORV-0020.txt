Vantro Trading: operational update on Orvan cargoes

[P1] Vantro Trading reports that its Orvan cargoes continue to move by rail and road where sea space is unavailable.

[P2] The firm says documentation and insurance for rail cargoes are taking longer than usual.

[P3] "We are working through the backlog with our partners," a company spokesperson said.

[P4] The update covers the firm's own operations and does not speak for the port or for other traders. It says rail and road cargoes are moving, but it does not give volumes or delivery times. The firm has added staff to handle documents and insurance, and it has asked customers to allow more time for each shipment. It expects the arrangement to last while the sea corridor remains closed. The statement was issued to customers and posted on the firm's notice board.

[P5] The notice board update is dated the same day as the firm's weekly call with customers. It lists the documents that rail cargoes now need and the extra time to allow for each check. It does not give a date for the return of the sea route, and it does not promise compensation for late delivery. Customers have been asked to send queries to the operations desk.

[P6] Vantro expects the rail route to remain its main alternative while the sea corridor is closed.
